# A co-professional's time is a commitment, not availability

Sessions can have more than one professional, but a session belongs to the availability slot of exactly one, its owner. A co-professional joining at a time they never opened as availability takes on a commitment: the session shows on their calendar and makes them busy, without becoming part of their availability. Availability keeps meaning only "a time clients can book me", so the owner's sessions always sit inside the owner's availability, and only co-professionals can be in a session outside their own.

## Considered Options

- **Create an availability for the co-professional when they accept.** Rejected: it would appear in Agendar for clients to book, so it would need hiding or deleting again, and the co-professional's real open hours would become indistinguishable from someone else's session.

## Consequences

- Conflict checks for a professional look at their own sessions and their commitments.
- When a co-professional accepts, any public slot of theirs at that exact time is closed for that date, and reopened if they leave.
